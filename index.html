NVIDIA's Nemotron 3.5: Open Agentic Models From Data Center to 4GB Edge

On Thursday NVIDIA unveiled three open-weight Nemotron 3.5 models aimed at agentic AI, spanning enterprise servers to local hardware. The flagship, Nemotron 3.5 Super VL, is a 120-billion-parameter mixture-of-experts model (12 billion parameters active per token) built with the Nemotron Coalition — Mistral AI, Thinking Machines Lab, Perplexity, and Black Forest Labs — for always-on agents that read text, images, documents, and video. NVIDIA claims it is the fastest open agentic model in its class — up to 5× faster throughput than the next-best open model — and matches the accuracy of its largest reasoning model at roughly one-fifth the parameters. The two smaller models bring agents to local hardware: Bolt 74B-A9B 24GB uses native 2-bit quantization to fit in 24GB of memory on RTX laptops and workstations, while Bolt E4B 4GB targets conversational and physical agents on GeForce RTX, Jetson, and RTX/DGX Spark systems, arriving October 23. All three ship with open weights, open datasets, and training recipes on Hugging Face, OpenRouter, and ModelScope.
